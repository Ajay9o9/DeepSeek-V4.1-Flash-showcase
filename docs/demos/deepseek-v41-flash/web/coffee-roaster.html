Brew guide

Full guide →
01

Weigh & grind

18g of coffee, ground medium-fine. Aim for the texture of table salt.

02

Bloom

Pour 50g of water at 96°C. Wait 30 seconds for the coffee to degas.

03

Pour

Slowly add water in circles until you reach 300g total. Keep it steady.

04

Enjoy

Total brew time: 2:30–3:00. Let it cool slightly, then taste the difference.
