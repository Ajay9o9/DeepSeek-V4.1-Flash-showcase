Digitization programme

We photograph the whole object, not just its face.

Since 2019, our conservation imaging lab has been capturing every catalogued object at archival resolution — recto, verso, edges, and raking-light detail. Each scan is colour-managed, embedded with technical metadata, and released under an open licence.

The result is a public record that can be studied, cited, and re-used without ever touching the original. Where the original is too fragile to travel, the scan travels for it.
