03 — Key Findings

Featured Finding · Nature Biotech 2024

A generalizable lattice framework for de novo cytokine mimetics.

We demonstrate that a geometric deep learning model trained on 180,000 protein structures can design functional IL-2 mimetics with tunable receptor selectivity. The resulting candidates show 40-fold bias toward the βγ receptor over the α subunit, reducing vascular leak toxicity in murine models while preserving anti-tumor immunity.
