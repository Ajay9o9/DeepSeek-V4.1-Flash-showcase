Hierarchy isn't about making things bigger — it's about deciding what the reader should notice first, second, third. Marguerite's point about "quiet contrast" landed: a 1.5× jump often reads more confidently than a 3× one.

Try the exercise again with a single typeface and only weight + size to differentiate. The restraint forces the composition to do the work.

Both, honestly. The ratio is a starting point — 1.4 to 1.6 is where most editorial work lives. But the feeling is what you're actually designing for. If the page reads as one voice with a clear lead, you've got it.
