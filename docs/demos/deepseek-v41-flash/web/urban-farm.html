Weekly Share · CSA

Your share of the harvest, every Thursday.

A seasonal box of whatever the farm is growing best that week — picked the morning it arrives. Choose a size, pause anytime, and pick up at one of six neighborhood hubs.

  • 18–22 week season, June through October
  • Free delivery to pickup hubs in Brooklyn & Queens
  • Swap items, skip weeks, or gift a share
  • Members get first access to workshops & events
