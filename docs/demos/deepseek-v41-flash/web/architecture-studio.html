Our Approach

We design spaces that breathe — where light, material, and silence converge.

FORM/NOIR is an architecture studio based in Copenhagen and Kyoto. We believe that architecture is not about filling space, but about framing it — creating moments of pause, reflection, and clarity.

Our work spans residential, cultural, and public projects across four continents. Each project begins with a single question: what does this place need to become?
