Reading list: Books for a slow autumn

Six titles for the season of long shadows and second cups of tea. Chosen by our poetry buyer, June, who has a weakness for anything with a lighthouse on the cover.

  1. The Salt Almanac
    Ines Marchetti
    A novel about the letters we don't send and the tides that keep returning them.
