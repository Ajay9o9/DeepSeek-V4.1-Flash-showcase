Accessibility

Theatre for
everyone.

Step-Free Access

Full step-free access to all public areas, including the auditorium, bar and restrooms. Accessible parking bays are located directly outside the main entrance.

Assisted Listening

Infrared assisted listening devices are available free of charge from the box office. All performances feature open captions on select dates.

Relaxed Performances

Dedicated relaxed performances for every production, with adjusted lighting and sound levels, a chill-out space and freedom to move around the auditorium.
