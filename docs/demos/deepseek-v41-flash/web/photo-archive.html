Photographer Notes

On process

Vol. 07
Mira Okonkwo — Lagos, 2024
"I don't photograph what I see. I photograph what I remember seeing, which is always something else entirely."

When I began the Coastal Light series, I thought I was making a document of place. Three years later, I understand it as a document of attention — a record of the hours I spent waiting for the light to do something I hadn't seen before.

The archive is not a container for images. It is a way of thinking about how images accumulate meaning over time, how a photograph taken in 2021 reads differently in 2024, how context is not something we add but something that happens to us.

Mira Okonkwo
Contributing Photographer
