02 / Call for Papers

Submit your work. Shape the conversation.

We invite original research, position papers, and provocations across eight interdisciplinary tracks. Submissions are peer-reviewed by a panel of 60 researchers from 22 countries.

We welcome work in progress, negative results, and methodological reflections alongside traditional papers. Extended abstracts of 1,500 words are due by June 30, 2025. Accepted contributions will be published in the open-access proceedings.

An industrial complex turned creative hub on the banks of the Tagus. The conference occupies three converted warehouses, a rooftop terrace, and a garden courtyard — all within walking distance of Lisbon's historic center.
