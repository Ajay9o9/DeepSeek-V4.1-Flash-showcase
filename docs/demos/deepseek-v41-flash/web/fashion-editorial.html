Each piece in Collection No. 09 is cut, sewn and finished in our Florence atelier. We work in small runs — never more than ninety of any garment — so that every seam is given the time it deserves.

— 01

Natural fibres

Wool crepe from Biella, silk satin from Como, cotton poplin from Albi. Nothing synthetic, nothing blended, nothing rushed.

Sourced in Italy
— 02

Hand-finished

Every hem, every buttonhole, every interior seam is finished by hand. The inside of a garment matters as much as the outside.
