Project Overview

Scope narrative · issued 2024-10-14

The Riverside Logistics Hub Phase II expands the existing distribution campus with three tilt-up concrete buildings totaling 184,000 SF, plus a shared truck court, stormwater detention, and utility extensions along Harbor Way.

This bid package covers core & shell only. Tenant improvements, racking, and MEP fit-out will be bid separately under Package #2024-119. The successful bidder will hold a GMP contract with Halden & Co. as construction manager.

Site work is substantially complete. Foundations are poured and cured. Structural steel is on-site and staged in the laydown yard. The awarded contractor is expected to mobilize within 14 days of notice to proceed.
