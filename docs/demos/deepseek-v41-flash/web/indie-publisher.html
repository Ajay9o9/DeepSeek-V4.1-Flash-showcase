Author Spotlight

Maren Voss on writing the coast

Maren Voss
"I write about places that are disappearing — not with nostalgia, but with urgency. The salt marsh doesn't need my elegies. It needs witnesses."

Maren Voss's debut novel, The Salt Road, follows three generations of a fishing family on the Oregon coast as they navigate the slow erosion of their livelihood and the faster erosion of their certainties. It's a book about what we inherit, what we lose, and what we choose to carry forward.

Submission Note

We're looking for voices that unsettle.

We read every submission that comes through our door. We're drawn to work that takes risks, that refuses easy resolution, and that trusts the reader to meet it halfway. If that sounds like your writing, we want to see it.

We accept simultaneous submissions, but please let us know if your work finds a home elsewhere. Response time is typically 8–12 weeks.

Read Submission Guidelines
01

What We Want

Literary fiction, narrative nonfiction, poetry collections, and essay collections that challenge convention.

02

How to Submit

Send the first 50 pages, a synopsis, and a brief author bio through our online portal.

03

What Happens Next

Our editorial team reviews every submission. If we're interested, we'll request the full manuscript.
