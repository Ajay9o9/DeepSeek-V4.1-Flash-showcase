02 / HOW TO PLAY

Three phases. One empire.

Iron Meridian runs on a tight three-phase loop. Learn it in ten minutes, master it over a hundred games.

01

Survey & Claim

Draft terrain tiles from the central market. Each tile you claim opens new routes and blocks your rivals' expansion.

02

Lay Track

Spend iron and labor to connect cities. Longer routes mean bigger payouts — but every rail you build is a promise you must defend.

03

Winter Reckoning

When the season turns, contracts expire and debts come due. Score your network, pay your barons, and prepare for the next year.
