What We Do

Full-stack robotics, from silicon to behavior.

We don't outsource the hard parts. Every layer of the stack is built and validated in-house.

01

Perception

Multi-modal sensing pipelines that fuse vision, depth, touch, and proprioception into a coherent world model.

02

Motion Planning

Sampling-based and optimization planners that operate in real time under uncertainty and dynamic constraints.

03

Learning & Control

Reinforcement learning and imitation policies that transfer from simulation to hardware with minimal fine-tuning.

Hardware & Integration
