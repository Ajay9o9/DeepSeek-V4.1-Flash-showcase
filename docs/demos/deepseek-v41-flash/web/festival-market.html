Venue plan

Riverside Park

Nestled along the Willamette River, our open-air layout is designed for wandering. Every zone is a short stroll from the next.

  • Main stage with river views
  • Dedicated food court & bar area
  • Three workshop tents with daily demos
  • Accessible pathways throughout
  • Bike parking & shuttle stop at entrance
