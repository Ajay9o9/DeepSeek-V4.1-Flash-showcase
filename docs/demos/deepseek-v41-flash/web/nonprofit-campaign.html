Project stories

Where the work meets the water.

Three projects, three coastlines, one shared belief: restoration works when it's led by the people who live there.

Restoration Palawan, Philippines

Rebuilding the mangrove nursery that saved a village

After Typhoon Odette flattened 40 hectares of protective mangrove, a women-led cooperative replanted 120,000 seedlings — and cut storm surge damage by an estimated 60% in the next season.

Science Monterey Bay, CA

Kelp forests are coming back

Three years of urchin removal and kelp outplanting have restored 340 hectares of canopy — and the sea otters noticed.
