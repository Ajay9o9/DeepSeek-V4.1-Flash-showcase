01 — Investment Thesis

We invest where conviction meets patience.

Our approach is deliberately unfashionable. We avoid momentum, eschew leverage, and measure success in decades rather than quarters. The result is a portfolio built to endure.

i.

Concentrated Conviction

We hold no more than 50 positions across the entire firm. Every dollar is allocated to a business we have studied for years — not a screen we ran for an afternoon.

ii.

Structural Advantage

We seek businesses with durable moats: pricing power, switching costs, network effects, and regulatory barriers that compound over time rather than erode.

iii.

Aligned Stewardship

Our partners' capital sits alongside our own. We do not charge performance fees on unrealized gains, and we do not sell to meet quarterly targets.

05 — Sustainability

Returns that endure.

We believe that businesses which respect their employees, communities, and environment are more likely to compound value over decades. Sustainability is not a constraint on returns — it is a source of them.

Our portfolio companies employ over 84,000 people across 19 countries. We engage with management teams on governance, labor practices, and environmental impact — not as activists, but as long-term owners who care about the next 30 years.

In 2025, we became a signatory to the UN Principles for Responsible Investment and published our first standalone sustainability report. We have also committed to net-zero portfolio emissions by 2045, with interim targets set for 2030.

We do not exclude sectors wholesale. Instead, we invest in the businesses best positioned to lead the transition — and we hold them accountable to measurable outcomes.
