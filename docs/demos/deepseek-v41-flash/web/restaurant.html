Meet the chef

Elena Marchetti

Born in Puglia and trained in Modena, Chef Elena brings a deep respect for simplicity and the seasons. Her cooking is rooted in memory — the scent of olive groves, the sound of the Adriatic, the generosity of the table.

At Casa Oro, she works with a small team of artisans to source the finest ingredients from local producers and small family farms across the Mediterranean.
