Venue

Kraftwerk Berlin

A former power plant turned iconic event space in the heart of Berlin. Industrial bones, modern comfort.

Where industry meets energy.

Kraftwerk Berlin is a 1960s power plant transformed into one of Europe's most distinctive event venues. With 3,000 m² of raw industrial space, soaring ceilings, and state-of-the-art AV, it's the perfect backdrop for a conference that's anything but ordinary.

Köpenicker Str. 70, 10179 Berlin 5 min from Heinrich-Heine-Straße U-Bahn
