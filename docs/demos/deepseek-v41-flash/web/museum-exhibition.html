Artist Statement

On making room
for what isn't said.

Mira Okonkwo
b. 1985, Enugu · Lives in Lisbon

I work with clay because it remembers. Every fold, every thumbprint, every hesitation in the wrist is preserved — a fossil of a moment that would otherwise be lost.

This exhibition gathers ten years of that remembering. The vessels are not containers; they are witnesses. They hold the shape of the silence that surrounded their making.

Mira Okonkwo's practice spans ceramics, installation, and sound. Her work has been shown at the Venice Biennale, the Serralves Museum, and the Metropolitan Museum of Art. She is the recipient of the 2022 Joan Miró Prize and a 2024 MacArthur Fellowship. The Shape of Silence is her first major solo exhibition in North America.
