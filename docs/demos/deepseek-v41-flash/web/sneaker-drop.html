The Story
"We didn't set out to make a sneaker. We set out to make a feeling."
Designer
Mara Chen
Development
18 months
Prototypes
47 iterations

The AERO GLIDE 01 began as a sketch on a napkin during a red-eye flight from Tokyo to Portland. Mara Chen, our lead designer, was frustrated. Every sneaker on the market felt like a compromise — too heavy, too stiff, too safe.

She wanted something that felt like running on air. Something that disappeared on your foot the moment you laced up. Something that looked as fast as it felt.

Eighteen months and forty-seven prototypes later, the AERO GLIDE 01 was born. It's not just a sneaker. It's a statement. It's a refusal to accept the status quo. It's the feeling of flight, captured in 218 grams of engineered perfection.

This is Drop 01. There will be others. But there will never be another first.
