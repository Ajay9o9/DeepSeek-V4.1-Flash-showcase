Our Story

We believe furniture should be inherited, not replaced.

FURNITURE / SHOWROOM began in a small Copenhagen workshop in 2012. We were tired of disposable design — pieces that looked good in a photograph but fell apart in a decade. So we started making furniture the slow way: solid joinery, natural finishes, and a repair guarantee that lasts a lifetime.

Today, we work with a team of 14 craftspeople across two studios. Every piece is still made by hand, one at a time.
