01 / Installation

A room that listens back.

Room 04 — Threshold Fig. 01

Eleven rooms. One continuous breath.

The exhibition unfolds as a single unbroken passage — no doors, no signage, no exit until the final chamber. Each room is tuned to a specific frequency, from the low hum of the entry corridor to the crystalline silence of the last.

Visitors move at their own pace. There is no correct route. The architecture responds to presence: light shifts, sound bends, fog thickens and thins around the body.

"You are not looking at the work. The work is looking at you."

Commissioned by the Rotterdam Light Biennale and produced over eighteen months with a team of twelve engineers, two composers and one perfumer.

02 / Artist

On making a room remember.

Mira Halvorsen
Lead Artist · b. 1987, Bergen

Mira Halvorsen works at the intersection of light, sound and architecture. Her installations have been shown at the Venice Biennale, Hamburger Bahnhof and the Mori Art Museum. She lives and works in Rotterdam.

"I wanted to build a place where the visitor's own body becomes the instrument — where attention is the only material that matters."

I have spent the last decade making rooms that behave less like galleries and more like weather systems. The work is not an object to be seen; it is a condition to be entered. IMMERSIVE / EXHIBITION is the largest iteration of this idea I have attempted — eleven rooms, each tuned to a different register of perception.

The piece began with a simple question: what happens when we remove every cue that tells a visitor how to behave? No labels, no benches, no arrows. What remains is a body in space, listening. The rooms do the rest.

I am grateful to the engineers, composers and collaborators who built this with me. And to you, for walking in.

— Mira Halvorsen, Rotterdam, 2025

04 / Sensory Notes

What to expect.

Light

Stroboscopic sequences in Rooms 03 and 07. Flashing at 4–8 Hz. Not suitable for visitors with photosensitive epilepsy.

Intensity

Sound

Continuous low-frequency tones (28–60 Hz) and sudden dynamic peaks up to 95 dB. Ear protection available at the entrance.

Intensity

Scent

A bespoke fragrance diffused throughout — cedar, ozone and wet stone. Low-allergen, but present in every room.

Intensity

Space

Uneven floors, narrow passages and one room with a 4-metre drop. Step-free route available on request.
