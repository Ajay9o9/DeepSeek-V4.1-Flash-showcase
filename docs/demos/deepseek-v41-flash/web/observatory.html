Featured Study

A New Class of Pulsating White Dwarfs in the Kepler Field

Using 18 months of continuous photometry from the Kepler Array, our team has identified a previously unknown class of variable white dwarfs with pulsation periods between 200 and 400 seconds. These objects may represent a missing link in stellar evolution.

Visitor Program

Book a night at the summit

Our visitor program runs year-round, weather permitting. Sessions include a guided tour of the control room, hands-on time with our outreach telescopes, and a hot meal at the mid-level station.

  • Small groups of 12 or fewer
  • Professional astronomer guides
  • All-weather gear provided
  • Complimentary astrophotography
