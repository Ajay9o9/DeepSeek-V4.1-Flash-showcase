Our delivery promise

Three things we'll never compromise on — because good food shouldn't be a gamble.

Next-day, cold-chain delivery

Order by 8pm and we'll bring it tomorrow evening in insulated, recyclable crates. Everything stays at the right temperature from farm to doorstep.

Grown within 120 miles

Every item is traceable to a named farm. We pay our growers a fair, published price — and we tell you exactly who grew your dinner.

Not happy? We'll make it right

If anything arrives less than perfect, tell us within 24 hours. We'll refund it or replace it on your next delivery — no photos, no fuss.
